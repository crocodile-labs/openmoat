### Security
- Refusals made before a credential is verified no longer write one audit row each: `openmoat proxy`'s and `openmoat run`'s `407` (`proxy-session`), a request head the proxy cannot read (`proxy-request`) and a hook relay call with a wrong token (`run-guard-token`) are recorded at once the first time, then counted, with one row per 10 seconds per kind that says how many it stands for. Before, any local process could grow the audit log without limit until it could no longer be written, after which the proxy and the guard refused everything (ADR-070). `openmoat-proxy` exports the counter as `coalesce`.
