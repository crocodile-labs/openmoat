- **Breaking** `openmoat-core`, `openmoat-hosts` and `openmoat-sdk`: the `ask` verdict is `review` (ADR-053). `Verdict::Ask` is `Verdict::Review` (and `Verdict::as_str` gives `"review"`), `Policy::ask`/`repo_ask` and `RepoPolicy::ask` are `review`/`repo_review`, `PolicyError` gains `OldAndNewName`, `Policy` gains `old_names` (`OldNames`), `openmoat_hosts::Asks` and `HostSpec::asks` are `Reviews` and `HostSpec::reviews` (with `on_no_prompt` and `defer_is_review_on_no_prompt`), `HookEvent::PreToolUseNoAsk` is `PreToolUseNoPrompt`, `bench::Answer::Ask` is `Answer::Review`, `openmoat_audit::Summary::asked` is `reviewed` and `reviews_per_active_hour_centi`; the SDK's `Verdict::Ask` is `Verdict::Review`. Serialised verdicts are `"review"`; `"ask"` is still read. No deprecated aliases are kept ([#PR](https://github.com/crocodile-labs/openmoat/pull/PR)).
