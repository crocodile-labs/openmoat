### Added
- Live audit delivery to a SIEM or log collector: `~/.moat/delivery.yaml` names up to four sinks, OTLP/HTTP logs (JSON) or an HTTPS webhook whose bodies are signed with HMAC-SHA-256 (`X-OpenMoat-Signature`). The hook never waits for it: after answering, the guard starts a detached delivery run, which sends the events after each sink's place in the audit log, every text masked again. A sink that fails is tried again with growing waits; one that answers with an error trying again cannot fix is paused; one that falls more than `max_backlog` events behind is told which events it missed, and the skip is recorded in the audit log (`audit-delivery-overflow`). New commands `openmoat audit deliver status`, `test` and `flush` (`docs/DELIVERY.md`, ADR-081).
