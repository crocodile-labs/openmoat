### Added
- When OpenMoat denies a call or holds it for review, the agent and you now read what was stopped and what you can do, in every agent: the rule's title, the call (secrets masked), the line earlier releases sent, why the rule exists, one next step for you, and `openmoat why --last` with the rule's page (ADR-059). Rules without a title, and OpenMoat's own checks, keep the one line ([#751](https://github.com/crocodile-labs/openmoat/pull/751)).
- Policy rules can say `title:` and `why:`; every deny and review rule of the default policy has both, and `openmoat policy upgrade` adds them to your policy. `openmoat policy lint` names the deny and review rules without a title ([#751](https://github.com/crocodile-labs/openmoat/pull/751)).
- `openmoat watch` follows your agents' calls as they happen, one line each, in a second terminal pane: what ran, what was denied and what waits for you; `--host`, `--only deny|review`, `--session`, `--no-follow`. It reads the audit log only ([#751](https://github.com/crocodile-labs/openmoat/pull/751)).
- `openmoat why` explains one decision (`--last`, an event id or a time): the rules that decided it and where they are in your policy, how your policy decides each part of the call now, the session's context, and how to change it; `--format json` ([#751](https://github.com/crocodile-labs/openmoat/pull/751)).
