### Fixed
- `openmoat policy upgrade` could refuse to upgrade a policy started from an older default with an edit near the top of a rule list ("the upgraded policy does not lint"): its line merge lined one list up with another and added a second `review:` header. It now merges each top-level key's own lines and each rule (`- id:`) with the same one in the new default: rules the default adds or moves arrive in its order, rules you added stay after the rule they followed, and only a rule both you and the default changed is left for you, as before ([#754](https://github.com/crocodile-labs/openmoat/pull/754)).
