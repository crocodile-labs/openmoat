### Security
- `env-dump` now denies printing other processes' environments with `ps`: the BSD modifier `e` in an option word without a dash (`ps eww`, `ps auxeww`, `ps e -p 1`), macOS's `-E` (`ps -E`, `ps -axwwE`) and an environment column in a format list, and Solaris and illumos `pargs -e`. Option values are not read as options, and `ps -e`, `ps -ef` and `ps aux` (every process) are unchanged. Reads of `/proc/<pid>/environ` were already denied; fixtures now cover another process's file, a glob, a `..` path and `xargs -0` ([#848](https://github.com/crocodile-labs/openmoat/pull/848)).
