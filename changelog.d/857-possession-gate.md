### Security
- `openmoat gate setup` turns on a second factor for every command that loosens the protection: once a person enrols an authenticator app (TOTP, RFC 6238), `allow`, `trust`, `edit` and `secret add` that widen, `doctor --accept` and `init` over drift, `hooks accept`, `mcp accept`, `mcp unwrap`, `policy trust-key`, `policy upgrade --accept-widening` and `--undo`, `policy install` and `remove` that widen, `audit checkpoint --new-key`, `uninstall`, `proxy uninstall`, `approvals connect` and `approvals allow` also ask for a code; `--yes` never skips it, a wrong or reused code refuses (exit 64), and five wrong codes in 15 minutes lock it for the rest of the window. The secret is owner-only in `~/.moat/audit.key.gate`, denied to agents by `kernel-self`. `openmoat gate status` and `openmoat gate remove` check and turn it off (ADR-117) ([#857](https://github.com/crocodile-labs/openmoat/pull/857)).
- Every loosening is recorded in the audit log under the session `policy-changes`, gate on or off: the command, what it changed (each case ADR-111's check finds it moves toward allow), the account, and whether a code was given; each refusal at the gate too ([#857](https://github.com/crocodile-labs/openmoat/pull/857)).
