### Added
- Team mode without a server: `openmoat team join|update|leave` installs a team's signed bundle (rules and required settings, kept in a repository or an MDM-managed folder) as a layer over your policy that only narrows; `openmoat inventory [--format json|ocsf]` reports one machine's agents, hooks and their liveness, protection levels, MCP pins, policy, lock and latest checkpoint, redacted; `--check <bundle>` exits 1 on drift for a scheduled job; `--deliver` sends the OCSF events to delivery sinks that say `inventory: true` (docs/TEAM.md, ADR-119) ([#858](https://github.com/crocodile-labs/openmoat/pull/858)).
