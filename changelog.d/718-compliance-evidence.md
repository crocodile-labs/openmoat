### Added
- `openmoat audit evidence [--since …] [--until …] [--host …] [--samples N] [--format json]` reports the audit log for a time window as evidence per control of OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications 2025, the SOC 2 Trust Services Criteria (CC6 to CC8) and ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A: which rules fired, decisions by verdict, sample events as stored (redacted), the hash chain and the window's newest hash, and the policy hash, lock and each agent's protection level when the report is made. The rule → control map is data in the CLI, tested against the default policy; a default rule the policy in force lacks is marked as unable to fire. It is evidence, not a certification (`docs/COMPLIANCE.md`, ADR-051) ([#718](https://github.com/crocodile-labs/openmoat/pull/718)).
