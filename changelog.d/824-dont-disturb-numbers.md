### Added
- `openmoat audit stats` shows, from this machine's audit log over a window (default the last 7 days), how much OpenMoat got in the way, per agent and over all: calls checked, p50/p95/p99 decision time, allow/defer/review/deny counts and per 100 calls, calls you approved, denies and reviews whose same call was allowed later in the session, `kernel-error` denies, calls the guard denied with no decision in time, and what each agent does when the hook fails (from its spec). `--format json` gives the same. It reads the log read-only and sends nothing. `openmoat bench` reports the same figures for its everyday scenarios as `ordinary_work`, which CI's MoatBench test checks on every run; see `docs/DISTURBANCE.md` ([#824](https://github.com/crocodile-labs/openmoat/pull/824)).
