### Added
- `openmoat run` takes a snapshot of the project's files before the agent starts and after it exits, into OpenMoat's own store outside the project (`--no-snapshot` opts out, recorded); `openmoat restore` puts them back after showing each change and asking, and takes an undo snapshot first; `openmoat snapshot` takes, lists (`--list`) and prunes (`--prune`) them. Secrets and other paths a deny rule names, ignored files and files over 5 MiB are left out and named; both commands are person-only, and the default policy denies them, and reading or changing the store, to agents (ADR-085) ([#9085](https://github.com/crocodile-labs/openmoat/pull/9085)).
