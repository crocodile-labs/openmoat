### Added
- While `openmoat approvals listen` runs, a call held for review waits up to 20 seconds in the hook for the approver's answer, so an approval from the phone runs the call at once instead of on the next try, and a deny denies it at once. The wait stays below the guard's 30-second budget and below each agent's hook time limit as its spec records it (a quarter of it where a timed-out hook lets the call run); no answer in time, or no listener, leaves the review as before. `openmoat approvals wait <seconds>` sets it, 0 turns it off ([#9404](https://github.com/crocodile-labs/openmoat/pull/9404)).
