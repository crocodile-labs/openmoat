### Added
- `openmoat init --hooks-only` writes and pins the agents' hooks and nothing else (no agent sandbox, no MCP step, no opt-in question); over a readable lock the other agents' pins stay as they are, and without one every installed hook is pinned. `openmoat init --format json` prints one document for scripts and asks no question, so it needs `--hosts`, `--yes` or `--dry-run` (from a contribution by Marlon Costa, [#846](https://github.com/crocodile-labs/openmoat/pull/846)).
